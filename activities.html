Farm Activities & Attractions

There is plenty to keep families and visitors entertained all day long!

Corn Maze

Test your navigation skills in our annual summer corn maze challenge.

Hayrides & Pumpkin Picking

Enjoy seasonal tractor hayrides and pick your own Pumpkins in autumn.
