Farm Shop Produce

We stock a wide variety of fresh local produce grown right in our gardens or sourced nearby:


Fresh Eggs & Dairy

Free-range eggs laid daily by our farm chickens, fresh milk, artisan yogurt, and local cheese.

Bakery & Award-Winning Pies

Pies with apricot/blackberry fillings, homemade breads, cakes, and hand-made chocolates

Locally sourced fruit juices, regional ciders, craft beers, and local honey.
