About Abbington Farm Park

Located in the heart of Scotland between Edinburgh and Carlisle, Abbington Farm has been converted into avibrant visitor attraction designed for families, garden enthusiasts, and travellers looking for a quality stop.

Our History

What started as a working farm has grown into a family-friendly estate with a shop selling local produce, an attached cafe, a small petting zoo, a play park, and scenic walks.

Local & Sustainable

Our pork is field-reared right here on the farm. Our chickens lay fresh free-range eggs daily, and our garden supplies fresh vegetables for the shop.

Our Community Mission

We aim to welcome local residents, day-trippers, and tourists with a fun yet professional atmosphere that celebrates countryside living.
